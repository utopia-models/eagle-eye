# Founder one-shot

**The offer:** one buyer (a funded founder or head of engineering with a live post for an AI or platform engineer), one outcome (the thing that post is hiring for, working on a live link you host), a date (14 days), a guarantee (if they cannot click through a working version by day 14, they pay nothing), month to month after. They give you no access to get it (research/delivery-guarantee-copy.md).

## Steps
1. **Gate the target.** Funded within 90 days, 1-200 staff, the post names the work, the post text is saved whole. Fail any and stop.
2. **Build their piece first.** (research/technical-buyer-outreach.md section 3) A working component for the roadmap item in the post, on a link, from public or sample data so it runs without them doing anything. See playbooks/upwork-oneshot.md steps 2-4 (same build and review loop).
3. **Make the page.** One URL per founder, noindex, no login. Order: outcome headline, the video, why we reached out (their post), the component running, proof (each item labelled for what it is), offer line with price and qualifier ("for funded teams with an open role who can spend this much a month"), calendar call to action, short FAQ. Template: templates/offer.md.
4. **Make the video.** Founder contract: their post on screen, the component, the diagram. The opener says it is already built and the link is theirs to click. Show the most valuable screen first. Name real protocols and real numbers from your own build, one workflow per claim (research/technical-buyer-outreach.md).
5. **Send.** playbooks/cold-apply-campaign.md touch schedule; the page link first appears in touch 2.
6. **Price anchor.** A side-by-side of a full-time hire's loaded cost (salary, tax, benefits, recruiter fee, months vacant, sourced per role) against the monthly fee. Never guess the hire's numbers.

## Standards
- Write to engineers: no hype words, no term you cannot explain, a number only when your build produced it.
- One decision per message.
- Proof is labelled honestly ("our own agent fleet", "a paid client project") and never implies a customer type we do not have.
- The offer is one thing. A menu of services is the failure this playbook exists to avoid.
