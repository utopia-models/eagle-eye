# Founder one-shot

**The offer:** one buyer (a funded founder or head of engineering with a live post for an AI or platform engineer), one outcome (the thing that post is hiring for, running on their staging), a date (14 days), a guarantee (working component on their staging in 14 days or the setup fee is refunded), month to month after.

## Steps
1. **Gate the target.** Funded within 90 days, 1-200 staff, the post names the work, the post text is saved whole. Fail any and stop.
2. **Build their piece first.** A working component for the roadmap item in the post, on a link, from public or sample data so it runs without them doing anything. See playbooks/upwork-oneshot.md steps 2-4 (same build and review loop).
3. **Make the page.** One URL per founder, noindex, no login. Order: outcome headline, the video, why we reached out (their post), the component running, proof (each item labelled for what it is), offer line with price and qualifier ("for funded teams with an open role who can spend this much a month"), calendar call to action, short FAQ. Template: templates/offer.md.
4. **Make the video.** Founder contract: their post on screen, the component, the diagram. No price, no question, no spoken link.
5. **Send.** playbooks/cold-apply-campaign.md touch schedule; the page link first appears in touch 2.
6. **Price anchor.** A side-by-side of a full-time hire's loaded cost (salary, tax, benefits, recruiter fee, months vacant, sourced per role) against the monthly fee. Never guess the hire's numbers.

## Standards
- Proof is labelled honestly ("our own agent fleet", "a paid client project") and never implies a customer type we do not have.
- The offer is one thing. A menu of services is the failure this playbook exists to avoid.
- Course citations (private): fazio lessons 03-offers/03-claims-guarantees, 03-offers/04-authority-evidence-case-studies, 10-dream-100.
