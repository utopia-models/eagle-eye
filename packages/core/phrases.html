Eagle phrases

Start a mode

  • Eagle, change request ... goan engineering change to a system; filed and mailed to Horus
  • Eagle, VSL for <job, company or topic>prep a take
  • Eagle, show <thing>open an Eagle Eye view, the OCC or the calendar
  • Eagle, statusthe one-line state, as a chip
  • Eagle, ask <lane> <what>a board request to that lane
  • Eagle, tell <lane> <what>a mail to that lane
  • Eagle, stophalt now and release the laptop
  • Eagle, conversationEagle speaks short answers until `that's all`
  • Eagle, quietback to DEFAULT (chimes and chips only)
  • Eagle, that's allends a conversation, back to DEFAULT

End or drop

  • gostarts what was collected
  • canceldrops what was collected
Voice or the text line: the same words. Confirmation is a chip, never speech. A half sentence with no go is never acted on. Source: phrases.tsv (knowledge#16048)
