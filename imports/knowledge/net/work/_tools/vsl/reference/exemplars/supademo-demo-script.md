## Beat sheet
1. Opening (about 10s): product, audience and the viewer's problem together; the first ten seconds carry the most weight.
2. Pain point (about 12s): the daily challenge, showing the current approach falls short; creates the gap.
3. Walkthrough step one (about 18s): one action tied to a benefit.
4. Walkthrough step two (about 18s): another single action tied to an outcome; focus on workflow, not feature list.
5. Turning point (about 15s): the moment the viewer sees it will help, with the value named explicitly such as time saved.
6. Call to action (about 10s): a specific next step doable immediately, not a vague "learn more".
7. Cut pass: time the read-aloud and delete anything not supporting the core message; put the most valuable content early.

The source gives five sections and total-length ranges only (1-2 min feature walkthrough, 3-4 min sales demo). Per-beat seconds here are my allocation to a 90s target.

## What to copy
- A distinct turning-point beat that states the value out loud.
- Single-action walkthrough steps, each tied to an outcome.
- A read-aloud timing and cut pass before recording.

## What not to copy
- The generic product-introduction opening it warns against; do not start with who the company is.
- The vague closing prompts; a client pitch needs one concrete ask.
