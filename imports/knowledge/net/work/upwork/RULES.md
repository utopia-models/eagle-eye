# Upwork rules: what we never do (read before ANY Upwork message, proposal, page or click)

Why: Upwork Trust & Safety issued a Circumvention warning (ticket #55841136, 2026-11-11). A second violation can restrict or permanently block the account. Tyler, 2026-11-13: "No sharing contact information... All of these need to be baked in to you." Evidence: [POLICY.md §1](POLICY.md) ("What triggered our warning") and [POLICY.md §4](POLICY.md) (automation and detection). The gate that enforces rules 1-9 is `net/work/_tools/upwork/presend_check.py` (run by `voice.py check --channel upwork`); a message that fails it is not sent.

## A. Before a contract exists (every proposal, every message to a client who has not hired us)

1. Never write an email address or phone number, and never ask the client for theirs, or for an office address ("send me the office address and your phone number" is what drew the warning; knowledge#12842). No "calendar invite" either: it needs their email.
2. Never put any link in a message or proposal except an upwork.com link. That includes our own sites, demo pages, GitHub, Loom or Drive.
3. Never link a page that shows contact details (email, phone, LinkedIn, calendar, booking button) and never link a page whose call to action reads "email me" or "book a call". Linked pages are scanned too; the only call to action is "reply here on Upwork".
4. Never offer or accept a video-meeting, calendar or booking link (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, Skype, Calendly, cal.com).
5. Never mention or suggest Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal, Discord, LinkedIn or any social handle.
6. Never move the conversation off Upwork: no "email me", "text me", "call me", "DM me", "my number", "my email", "off Upwork", "outside Upwork". "When we get on a call, I'll have several questions" is fine: it names a call inside Upwork, not a way to reach you (Tyler 2026-11-17: the Mohamed copy stays).
7. Calls happen only through Upwork's own meeting tool (Schedule a Meeting / Upwork video call), set up and confirmed inside the room. The word "meeting" is fine, and so is "when we get on a call" in a letter (the call is Upwork's own); the bare word "meet" is blocked because it usually precedes a Meet link. No in-person meeting before a contract ("in person", "come by", "at your office"): Upwork reads it as moving the relationship off the platform.
8. Never talk about payment off the platform, at any stage. Contracts, milestones and invoices go through Upwork only.
9. Before sending, run `python3 wi/work/_tools/voice/voice.py check --agent horus --channel upwork --to <client> --name <first name> <file>` until it prints PASS. It runs `presend_check.py` with `--contract none`.

11. Still no personal contact details: no email, phone, social handle or chat-app handle, even for the client we are working for.
