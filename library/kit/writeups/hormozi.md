## How to run it like Hormozi
1. **Pick the market first.** A market that is in pain, can pay, is reachable and is growing beats a better offer to a poor one.
2. **Score the offer on four levers.** Raise the result the buyer wants and their belief they will get it; cut the wait and the work.

> Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice) (kit:hormozi-mit/claude-skill-hormozi-offer-audit/skills/audit/SKILL)

3. **Build the offer from obstacles.** Write the outcome, list what stands in the way, turn each obstacle into a deliverable, then drop what costs much and adds little.
4. **Reverse the risk** with a guarantee you can honour, and add bonuses that remove a named objection.
5. **Get leads** in the order warm outreach, content, cold outreach, paid ads; do more of what works before adding a channel.
6. **Sequence the money:** an offer that wins the customer, then an upsell, a downsell, and something that continues.
