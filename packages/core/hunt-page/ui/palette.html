Eagle Eye palette

One gold scale and one ink scale, 12 steps each, built on the Radix Colors method (amber + sand dark, tuned brighter on a true-black base). Every color in Eagle Eye is one of these 24 tokens, defined once in /opt/tokens.css. Types and statuses are told apart by shade, fill and icon, never by a second hue.
