EAGLE EYE

Privacy policy for the Eagle Eye mobile app (Android and iPhone). Updated 2026-11-18.

Eagle Eye is made by Utopia Models, Inc. The app shows you your own Eagle Eye: what your agents sent, what came back, and who answered. This page says exactly what it collects. The short version: your sign-in email and a push token. No tracking, no ads, no selling data.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email. You sign in with Google. We check the sign-in with Google, keep the email that Google confirms, and use it to find your Eagle Eye. An email that has no Eagle Eye gets "This Google account has no Eagle Eye yet." and nothing is stored about it.
  • A push token, if you allow notifications. It is a random address that Apple or Google gives your phone so we can tell you when a company or client answers or a call is booked. We store it with your Eagle Eye and the phone's platform (Android or iPhone).
  • Your Eagle Eye data the app shows: the lanes, companies and events your own agents recorded. It already belongs to you; the app only reads it for you. A person sees only their own page.

What stays on your phone

A sign-in token valid for 30 days, in the phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android). Signing out deletes it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no cross-app tracking.
  • No selling or sharing your data for marketing.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Who handles it

Google (sign-in and Android push), Apple (iPhone push), Cloudflare (the servers the app talks to). They process data only to do those jobs. Nothing else receives it.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your email and push token while you have an Eagle Eye. Signing out removes the token from your phone; switching notifications off stops pushes. To have your email and push token deleted from our servers, ask through utopiamodels.ai/call and we will delete them.

Children

Eagle Eye is a work tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes and the new version is here.
