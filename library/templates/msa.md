# Master services agreement

The structure follows the Common Paper Professional Services Agreement standard terms (CC BY 4.0, commonpaper.com): parties and effective date; services under each SOW; fees and payment; term and termination; ownership and licence of deliverables; confidentiality; warranties and disclaimer; limitation of liability; general terms.

The full clause text lives in `html/msa.html.j2` (about 7 KB, plain language). Off-platform clients only: a client found through a marketplace is covered by the marketplace's own contract terms, and this document must not be sent there (research/upwork-contract-terms.md).

This template is a starting point written by non-lawyers from a public standard; have counsel review before relying on it for a large engagement.
