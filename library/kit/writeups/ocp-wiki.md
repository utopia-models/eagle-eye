# ocp.wiki

Our own write-up of public pages by Max Forbang, read 2026-11-26. Some pages say they paraphrase others (the outbound
doctrine names Nick Saraev's cold email course); follow their source lines. The two long doctrines (cold email, grand
unified theory) were read in part verbatim and in part as a summary. Machine index: <https://ocp.wiki/llms.txt>; every
docs page also serves Markdown at the same path plus `.md`.

## Outbound strategy
<https://ocp.wiki/docs/marketing-and-sales/outbound-strategy-doctrine>
- A message that would still make sense sent to someone else is not personal enough.
- Give first, ask for a small step, show proof and authority.
- A message is four parts: personalisation, who you are, the offer, one call to action.
- Conversion rises with the value the buyer expects and with trust, and falls with friction.
- Build a system that learns, not a template.

## Cold email structure
<https://ocp.wiki/docs/marketing-and-sales/cold-email-structure-doctrine>
- The subject line does not name the product.
- Every touch stands on its own and asks for exactly one thing.
- No links or attachments before a reply.
- Measure positive replies per prospect, not opens.

## VSL structure
<https://ocp.wiki/docs/marketing-and-sales/vsl-structure-doctrine>
- Belief comes before purchase: pick one belief, one diagnosed market, one easy next step.
- Diagnose market sophistication and awareness before writing.
- For a high-ticket offer, sell the next step, leave price out, and give one call to action.

## Self-improving campaigns
<https://ocp.wiki/docs/marketing-and-sales/grand-unified-theory>
- Change only what currently limits results.
- With few samples, statistical learning may never switch on; design for that.
