## Beat sheet
1. Hook (0-5s): a recognised problem or wanted outcome; no company intro, no category explanation.
2. Audience and task (5-15s): names who the demo is for and the task they struggle with; sets the stakes.
3. First visible action (15-27s): one action toward the promised outcome; narration explains why it matters, not the UI label.
4. Second and third actions (27-40s): two more visible actions that continue to the outcome; proof by showing, not claiming.
5. Value link (40-52s): old way versus new way, with the claim limited to what was shown.
6. Single next step (52-60s): one action matched to where the viewer is in their journey.

Splitting the 15-40s block into beats 3 and 4 is my own division.

## What to copy
- A hook that opens on the viewer's problem, with no introduction.
- Narration that explains why, not what the interface says.
- Limiting the value claim to what the demo actually showed.

## What not to copy
- The 60-second total; adapt the proportions to 90s rather than the numbers.
- Generic "try the workflow" CTAs; use a specific booking or reply action.
