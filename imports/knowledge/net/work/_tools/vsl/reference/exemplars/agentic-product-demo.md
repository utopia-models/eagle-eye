## Beat sheet
1. Title card (about 2s): product name plus one tagline line; sets context, claims nothing.
2. Query step (about 3s): a real input is typed and run; proves the flow is the real one.
3. Results step (about 3s): rows appear in sequence and a counter settles; carries the "it works" proof.
4. Filter or refine step (about 3s): one toggle changes the data and the total recalculates; shows the product responding.
5. Publish or commit step (about 3s): the final action is clicked and a confirmation lands; this is the hero beat.
6. Outro fold-out (about 1s): scene returns to the dark background, leaving the finished result readable.
7. Pre-build shot-list gate: the author proposes the beat list and waits for confirmation before building, because a wrong flow cannot be fixed in the edit.

## What to copy
- One hero beat per phase; everything else stays quiet so the proof stands out.
- Walk the real flow in its real order, using wording taken from the live product.
- End on the finished result, held long enough to read.

## What not to copy
- Frame-count pacing and animation specifics; a spoken script needs seconds and narration, not frames.
- The Remotion dependency and its licence terms; they do not apply to a reused structure.
