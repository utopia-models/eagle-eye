# Delivery and guarantee copy

Pages fetched and read 2026-11-27. Quotes are word for word (25 words or fewer) and belong to their authors; the rest is our own words.

## The shape: result, date, consequence, short closer
> "If the prototype is not what you wanted within two weeks, walk away at no cost. No fees, no fight."

[RaftLabs](https://raftlabs.com/contact-us)

> "If you're not happy with the deliverable, you don't pay. No invoice, no awkward conversation, no strings."

[First Factory](https://firstfactory.com/risk-free-guarantee)

> "If you like what you see, we move to the full build with a clear scope and fixed price."

[RaftLabs, POC first](https://www.raftlabs.com/blog/poc-first-approach)

## Trial wording
> "If you're not completely satisfied, you won't be billed."

[Toptal's FAQ line, as quoted by a competitor](https://flexiple.com/alternatives/toptal); Toptal's own pages refused a scripted read.

## Show it before the price
> "We build a working prototype, show it to you live, then quote one price and deliver in ten working days."

[Vertikals](https://vertikals.ai/)

> "No deposit, nothing to pay until you see the first phase working."

[99 Francs](https://www.99francs.agency/)

## Delivery and ownership
> "Repositories, cloud accounts and credentials sit in your name from the first day"

[Gigabit](https://gigabit.agency/services/custom-software-development/)

> "All deliverables created by GitStart for you become your exclusive property."

[GitStart terms](https://gitstart.com/terms)

## How we apply it
Build the first version before asking for anything. Send a live link; ask for no access. State the guarantee as one condition, one date and one consequence:
"If you can't click through a working version by day 14, you pay nothing." Keep the day count and the after-trial terms identical everywhere they appear.

## Limits
- None of the pages read sells "you give us no access" as a benefit; that line is our own.
- Check each page again before quoting it to anyone: offers change.
