## 1. The offer a stranger will buy
- A buyer who already follows you will accept a vague offer; a stranger will not. Without an audience you need a
  first-purchase offer built for cold traffic ([0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sxjjIkIZ6Pg&t=0s)).
- Copying what an established seller offers their audience, then sending it cold, is the usual failure
  ([0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sxjjIkIZ6Pg&t=45s)).
- State the result the client wants (new customers, sales), with a number, not the service you perform or your
  experience ([5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sxjjIkIZ6Pg&t=317s)).
- Sell new money: leads, calls or sales the client was not getting. Improving something they already run draws a weak
  response and disqualifies everyone who does not run it ([4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUMR2FKPNww&t=274s)).
- Do not turn a narrow add-on for a tiny market into your front offer; mention it to the clients it fits
  ([6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUMR2FKPNww&t=412s)).
- Price at what this market will pay given the proof you have today
  ([10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUMR2FKPNww&t=638s)).

## 2. Risk reversal
A cold buyer is offered one specific result with the risk moved off them.

| shape | how it reads | where |
|---|---|---|
| guarantee | a set amount of revenue, clients, calls or leads, or the money back | [3:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oz58Tal2FeE&t=183s) |
| performance | a small technical fee that covers sending costs, then a fee per booked call | [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oz58Tal2FeE&t=229s) |
| pay as it happens | nothing up front, a fee for each appointment | [6:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sxjjIkIZ6Pg&t=407s) |

- The guarantee's first job is to get the call booked, before any close
  ([4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oz58Tal2FeE&t=276s)).
- Write conditions so a refund is owed only when the failure is yours: the client's minimum spend, availability for
  calls, a minimum close rate ([5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oz58Tal2FeE&t=321s)).
- Trade every concession. For a performance deal, get written permission to publish the results as a case study
  ([15:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oz58Tal2FeE&t=957s)).
- As case studies pile up, lower the guarantee ([12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oz58Tal2FeE&t=775s)).
- His beginner recommendation is performance-based cold email lead generation to businesses that sell to businesses
  ([15:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUMR2FKPNww&t=959s)).

## 3. With no case studies, show the product
- The gap is usually a missing description of what the client receives, more than missing proof
  ([1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oz58Tal2FeE&t=91s)).
- List every deliverable by name (pages, scripts, sequences, setup, launch) and show examples. He ranks a concrete
  stack without case studies above case studies with no stack
  ([9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oz58Tal2FeE&t=593s)).

## 4. First clients
1. Do not pick a niche first. Test combinations of a niche and an acquisition method, many and fast
   ([1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbAj7Yl_a-s&t=92s)).
2. With more time than money, start with cold outreach
   ([3:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbAj7Yl_a-s&t=230s)).
3. Choose obscure niches that are rarely pitched ([5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbAj7Yl_a-s&t=320s)).
4. Send a two-line personal message that uses the prospect's own words and ends by asking whether it is worth a chat.
   If you would not say it aloud, cut it ([6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbAj7Yl_a-s&t=411s)).
5. Send 20 a day to a new niche each day for 5 to 10 days, then compare replies
   ([8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbAj7Yl_a-s&t=503s)).
6. Once a niche answers, run a Dream 100: a free, researched deliverable for each of 100 prospects. He treats about 20
   calls and 3 to 5 clients as good ([11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbAj7Yl_a-s&t=683s)).
7. Add automated high-volume email after the first clients. He expects about one client per 5,000 to 10,000 emails and
   a spend from about $600 a month ([13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbAj7Yl_a-s&t=818s)).

## 5. What the prospect checks, and the call
- Interested people read your profile, site and sales video before they reply. The message only brings the visit; the
  offer, proof and guarantee on the page do the selling ([2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a76Y8q5EBqY&t=136s)).
- Make the sending domain resolve to a real page; people look it up
  ([5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a76Y8q5EBqY&t=316s)).
- On the call: ask about the business, say whether the result is realistic, show what they get, give the price, ask
  for the decision. Bring in the guarantee when doubt about the result is what stops them
  ([14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oz58Tal2FeE&t=865s)).

## 6. Keeping it running
- Expect many clients to get no result, especially ones with no offer that already works on strangers. He calls 20%
  monthly churn normal for such an agency ([21:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUMR2FKPNww&t=1275s)).
- Choose between two tactics by money earned per hour on each, measured
  ([22:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUMR2FKPNww&t=1321s)).
- Find the one broken step: no calls booked is the offer; calls that do not close is selling; clients leaving is
  delivery. Do not change a front offer that converts ([24:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUMR2FKPNww&t=1458s)).
